BTP Prototype & Research Project

Digital Twin for Monitoring Embodied Carbon in Concrete Construction

A live, interactive web system that tracks site carbon emissions in real time instead of post-construction estimates.

Civil & Structural Engineering • Real-Site Validation (Mahindra Eden Study)

1. The Problem & Who It Helps

Context

Concrete generates up to 80% of upfront construction carbon, but emissions are currently audited months after work is done.

  • Who It Helps: Site engineers, project managers, and sustainability auditors on active jobsites.
  • The Information Gap: Delivery challans and batching slips sit in physical files without real-time tracking.
  • The Solution: Replace static retrospective reports with a live Digital Twin that tracks carbon as pours occur.
Goal: Catch carbon overruns during construction while decisions can still be made.

2. How the Digital Twin Works

Architecture

The system maintains a synchronized digital mirror of the physical concrete structure through three pillars:

  • Rigorous Carbon Accounting: Multi-stage calculations covering materials (A1–A3), transport (A4), and equipment (A5).
  • Continuous Progression: Tracks milestone pours from foundations to slabs without double-counting.
  • Dynamic Recalculation: Instant recalculation upon new deliveries, with What-If scenario simulations.
Standard: Compliant with EN 15978 / ISO 21930 upfront carbon rules.

3. Step 1: Effortless Site Ingestion

Workflow

Site teams can record progress without complex setup or manual data entry burden.

  • Structured Excel Import: Drag-and-drop existing site spreadsheets or workbooks in one click.
  • Site Ticket Photo OCR: Snap a photo of a physical delivery challan or invoice. AI extracts confirmed quantities.
  • Audit Integrity: Ambiguous or non-material items are flagged and omitted to prevent distorted audits.
Zero friction: Converts paperwork straight into carbon ledger entries.

4. Step 2: Milestone Logs & S-Curve

Workflow

Monitor project carbon evolution stage by stage against the planned budget baseline.

  • Milestone Ledger: Independent records for each pour (Foundation, Substructure, Superstructure).
  • Interactive Status Control: Toggle stages between Completed, In Progress, and Scheduled in real time.
  • Continuous S-Curve: Live comparison of planned budget vs. actual cumulative carbon and projected total.
Clear visibility: Scheduled milestones show '-' until work begins.

5. Step 3: Real-World Validation & Benchmarks

Results

Tested and proven on real construction data from the Mahindra Eden Whole Life Carbon Study.

  • Real Project Scale: 55,480 m³ of M30 concrete and 5,125 tonnes of steel rebar accurately tracked.
  • Industry Benchmarking: Instant rating against CPWD standards, GRIHA Green targets, and ICE benchmarks.
  • Hotspot Analysis: Pinpoints cement and steel as primary targets for low-carbon mix substitution.
Outcome: Proven accuracy on real-world Indian high-rise construction.
